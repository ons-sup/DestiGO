About DestiGO

Discover first.
Book later.

DestiGO is not a booking platform. It's a place to explore destinations through the eyes of fellow travelers. Each country portal is independently curated — with hand-picked destinations, local experiences, and cultural insights that help you decide where to go next.

Built as a university project by Ahmed Ben Mim and Ons Chtioui.
