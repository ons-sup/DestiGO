Travel Highlights

Landscapes

Madrid's parks, Barcelona's coast, Seville's plazas, Valencia's gardens, the Alhambra, and Mallorca's islands offer striking scenery.

History

Roman ruins, Moorish palaces, Gothic cathedrals, modernist architecture, and historic old towns reveal Spain's deep layers.

Cuisine

Spanish food ranges from tapas and paella to jamón ibérico, churros, salmorejo, and regional specialties.

Practical Visitor Information

Best Seasons

Spring and autumn are generally comfortable for sightseeing in many regions. Summer suits coastal areas, while inland cities can be hot.

Getting Around

Major cities are connected by flights and high-speed trains. Within cities, metro, tram, bus, and walking are common ways to explore.

Local Etiquette

Dinner starts late, dress modestly when visiting churches, and enjoy a leisurely pace — meals and conversation are part of the experience.
